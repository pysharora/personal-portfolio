# Brainstorm

Turn `$ARGUMENTS` into a “How might we?” question. Generate varied ideas using at least two structured methods, then evaluate the strongest by audience value, personal authenticity, editorial fit, accessibility, effort, risk, and maintainability. Refine selected ideas into outcomes, first steps, and risks. After confirmation, save `.claude/output/brainstorm-{slug}.md` and suggest `/bmad-break` or `/quick-spec`.
