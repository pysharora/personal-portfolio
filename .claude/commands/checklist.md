# Checklist

Score PASS, FAIL, or N/A with evidence across requirements/value, architecture/traceability, content and personal-fact accuracy, theme and responsive behavior, accessibility, SEO/metadata, security/privacy, verification, CI, documentation, compatibility, and release/rollback. Write `.claude/output/checklist.md`. Any FAIL blocks implementation unless fixed or explicitly waived with rationale. Do not require nonexistent tests; require honest evidence appropriate to risk.
