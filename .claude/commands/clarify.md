# Clarify

Read `.claude/output/problem.yaml`, or use `$ARGUMENTS` inline. Scan value/scope, personal facts, content sources, UX flows, theme behavior, responsive behavior, accessibility, SEO, integrations, edge cases, constraints, and terminology. Rate each area clear, minor gap, or significant gap.

Ask no more than five questions, one at a time. Each explains risk, gives a recommendation, and offers concrete options. Record answers inline and in a `clarifications` audit trail. Do not silently decide significant product or identity questions.
