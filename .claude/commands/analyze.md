# Analyze

Require problem, architecture, and backlog; include principles and UX spec when present. Do not edit files. Check duplication, ambiguity, missing WHY, underspecification, principles violations, traceability gaps, priority drift, terminology drift, circular dependencies, theme/accessibility gaps, unsupported personal facts, and orphan tasks.

Report CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM, and LOW findings with evidence and remedies, plus a story-to-architecture-to-task traceability table. CRITICAL findings block progression.
